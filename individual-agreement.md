You sign through the CLA Assistant signing flow linked from the project's pull-request check. ## 1. Definitions

**“You” or “Your”** means the individual signing this Agreement. Where You make a grant under Section 3(c)(iii), references to grants by You include grants made by You in Your disclosed representative capacity on behalf of the relevant Rights Owner.

**“Rights Owner”** means an employer, client or other person that owns or controls relevant rights in a Contribution and has expressly authorised You to execute this Agreement and make the grants in Section 2 on its behalf as described in Section 3(c)(iii).

**“Contribution”** means code, tests, documentation or other material intentionally Submitted for inclusion in, or improvement of, the Material, including modifications, additions and material produced with AI tools. A Contribution need not qualify for copyright protection. The grants in Section 2 cover only the rights specified there; submission does not make You the owner of another person's rights.

**“Copyright”** means economic copyright and related rights in a Contribution, including database rights, for their full duration and any extensions or renewals. It excludes patents, trade marks and moral rights, which are dealt with separately.

**“Material”** means the software and documentation of the open-source projects that We publish under the Chroniton Labs name, including the repositories of the Chroniton Labs GitHub organisation at https://github.com/chronitonlabs, those repositories at any successor hosting location, and any other repositories We clearly identify as part of a Chroniton Labs project, wherever hosted. Where a Contribution is Submitted to a particular project, references to the Material to which it was Submitted mean that project.

**“Submit”** means intentionally communicate material to Us or Our representatives for inclusion in, or improvement of, the Material, including through pull requests, source control, issue trackers and project mailing lists. It excludes material conspicuously marked in writing as **“Not a Contribution”** and private correspondence not intended for inclusion. An agent or automated tool acting at Your direction may make the communication on Your behalf.

**“Submission Date”** means the date a Contribution is Submitted.

**“Effective Date”** means the date You sign this Agreement.

**“ASF”** means The Apache Software Foundation.

## 2. Grant of Rights

### 2.1 Copyright Assignment and Fallbacks

**(a) Assignment.** You hereby assign to Us all Your right, title and interest worldwide in Copyright that You own, and all Copyright that a Rights Owner has expressly authorised You to assign on its behalf under Section 3(c)(iii), in:

- Contributions Submitted on or after the Effective Date; and

Where You act for a Rights Owner, You make this assignment in Your disclosed representative capacity on behalf of that Rights Owner as well as making the grants that apply to Your own rights.

Assignment of existing rights takes effect on the Effective Date, not retrospectively. So far as permitted by law, this is also a present assignment of future Copyright, taking effect when the relevant rights exist and the Contribution has been Submitted. Rights that You or the represented Rights Owner later acquire in such a Contribution are included when acquired. You are not required to create or Submit future work. Copyright already owned by Us is not transferred again.

The licence includes rights to use, reproduce, adapt, modify, translate, arrange, combine, publish, distribute, make available, communicate to the public, display, perform and otherwise exploit the Contribution in source and object form, in all media and by all legally permissible methods of use.

You consent, personally or on the Rights Owner's behalf as applicable and to the extent permitted by law, to the transfers and sublicences contemplated by this Agreement, including grants to the ASF. If exclusivity is not valid, the licence is non-exclusive to the extent necessary to make it valid. Unknown methods of use are included only to the extent applicable law and the execution formalities permit.

**(c) Further fallback.** To the extent relevant economic rights covered by Section 2.1(a) can neither validly be assigned nor licensed, You consent, personally or on the Rights Owner's behalf as applicable, to the acts described in Section 2.1(b), and waive and agree not to assert those rights against Us, Our successors and direct or indirect licensees, only so far as that consent, waiver or agreement is lawful. This provision does not override mandatory author protections or supply rights for which You lack authority.

**(d) Licence back and retained freedom.** Immediately upon Our acquiring rights under this Section, We grant the person from whom We acquired those rights—You or the represented Rights Owner, as applicable—a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable licence for their full duration and any extensions or renewals, irrevocable to the extent permitted by law, to do all acts listed in Section 2.1(b), for any purpose and under any terms that person chooses.

For rights licensed rather than assigned, that person retains equivalent freedom to use and license them notwithstanding the exclusivity in Section 2.1(b).

The licence back and retained freedom cover only the Contribution and rights received from that person. They do not grant rights in other parts of the Material, third-party rights, or rights already owned by Us or another person. They do not override separate employment or services obligations.

**(e) Independent undertaking.** The grants in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 are not subject to automatic termination or reversion for breach of Section 2.3. Section 2.3 is an independently enforceable contractual undertaking. Subject to mandatory law, its breach does not automatically divest title or terminate rights already granted. Remedies against Us, including damages and applications for injunctive or other equitable relief where available, remain available.

**(f) Confirmatory documents.** On Our reasonable request and at Our expense, You will execute documents reasonably necessary to confirm rights already granted by You under this Agreement. Where You made a grant for a Rights Owner, You will use reasonable efforts to procure any confirmation reasonably required from that Rights Owner. Such documents may not enlarge the obligations or rights granted. This is a confirmatory obligation, not a requirement for further permission before We upstream a Contribution.

**(b) ASF exception.** We may grant to the ASF, and directly to recipients of software distributed by the ASF where its applicable instruments require, the copyright and patent licences and sublicences needed to contribute the Contribution for inclusion in an ASF project.

The ASF's exercise of rights validly granted under its instruments is not controlled by Our undertaking in Section 2.3(a). This exception does not authorise Us to sign another person's contributor agreement in that person's name.

**(c) Scope.** This undertaking applies to Our exercise of rights received under this Agreement and, as between You and Us, to original material You create and Submit whose Copyright is already owned by Us.

It does not convert third-party material into Our property, alter its existing licence, or restrict an independent recipient's exercise of rights under the Apache License, Version 2.0. We cannot avoid this undertaking merely by relying on an Apache licence to the same Contribution received from You.

Protection on a transfer of rights is set out in Section 6.3. A change in Our shareholders does not change Our identity or release Us from this Agreement.
