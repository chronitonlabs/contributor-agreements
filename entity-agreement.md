## 1. Definitions

**“You” or “Your”** means only the legal entity identified above and signing this Agreement. It does not automatically include affiliates or the individuals listed in Schedule A. Where You make a grant on another rights owner's behalf under Section 3(c), references to grants by You include grants made in that authorised representative capacity.

A Submission on Your behalf is made by a person identified in Schedule A acting with Your authority. At first signature, this includes their earlier authorised Submissions covered by Section 2.1(a).

**“Contribution”** means code, tests, documentation or other material intentionally Submitted for inclusion in, or improvement of, the Material, including modifications, additions and material produced with AI tools. A Contribution need not qualify for copyright protection. The grants in Section 2 cover only the rights specified there; submission does not make You the owner of another person's rights.

**“Copyright”** means economic copyright and related rights in a Contribution, including database rights, for their full duration and any extensions or renewals. It excludes patents, trade marks and moral rights, which are dealt with separately.

**“Material”** means the software and documentation of the open-source projects that We publish under the Chroniton Labs name, including the repositories of the Chroniton Labs GitHub organisation at https://github.com/chronitonlabs, those repositories at any successor hosting location, and any other repositories We clearly identify as part of a Chroniton Labs project, wherever hosted. Where a Contribution is Submitted to a particular project, references to the Material to which it was Submitted mean that project.

**“Submit”** means intentionally communicate material to Us or Our representatives for inclusion in, or improvement of, the Material, including through pull requests, source control, issue trackers and project mailing lists. It excludes material conspicuously marked in writing as **“Not a Contribution”** and private correspondence not intended for inclusion. An agent or automated tool acting at Your direction may make the communication on Your behalf.

**“Submission Date”** means the date a Contribution is Submitted.

**“Effective Date”** means the date Your authorised signatory signs this Agreement.

**“ASF”** means The Apache Software Foundation.

## 2. Grant of Rights

### 2.1 Copyright Assignment and Fallbacks

**(a) Assignment.** You hereby assign to Us all Your right, title and interest worldwide in Copyright that You own, and all Copyright that another rights owner has expressly authorised You to assign on its behalf under Section 3(c), in:

- Contributions Submitted on Your behalf on or after the Effective Date; and
- existing Contributions Submitted on Your behalf by the persons initially listed in Schedule A, not already governed as to the relevant rights by an earlier signed contributor agreement with Us.

Where You act for another rights owner, You make this assignment in Your authorised representative capacity on behalf of that owner as well as making the grants that apply to Your own rights.

Assignment of existing rights takes effect on the Effective Date, not retrospectively. So far as permitted by law, this is also a present assignment of future Copyright, taking effect when the relevant rights exist and the Contribution has been Submitted. Rights that You or the represented owner later acquire in such a Contribution are included when acquired.

You are not required to create or Submit future work. Copyright already owned by Us is not transferred again. Earlier Submissions by a person added to Schedule A later are covered only if the written update expressly identifies them.

**(b) Licence fallback.** To the extent Copyright covered by Section 2.1(a) cannot validly be assigned, You grant Us, for Yourself or on the rights owner's behalf as applicable, the broadest licence permitted by law: worldwide, exclusive except for the retained freedom under Section 2.1(d), royalty-free, transferable in accordance with Section 6.3 and sublicensable through multiple tiers, for the full duration of those rights and any extensions or renewals.

The licence includes rights to use, reproduce, adapt, modify, translate, arrange, combine, publish, distribute, make available, communicate to the public, display, perform and otherwise exploit the Contribution in source and object form, in all media and by all legally permissible methods of use.

You consent, for Yourself or on the rights owner's behalf as applicable and to the extent permitted by law, to the transfers and sublicences contemplated by this Agreement, including grants to the ASF. If exclusivity is not valid, the licence is non-exclusive to the extent necessary to make it valid. Unknown methods of use are included only to the extent applicable law and the execution formalities permit.

**(c) Further fallback.** To the extent relevant economic rights covered by Section 2.1(a) can neither validly be assigned nor licensed, You consent, for Yourself or on the rights owner's behalf as applicable, to the acts described in Section 2.1(b), and waive and agree not to assert those rights against Us, Our successors and direct or indirect licensees, only so far as that consent, waiver or agreement is lawful. This provision does not override mandatory author protections or supply rights for which You lack authority.

**(d) Licence back and retained freedom.** Immediately upon Our acquiring rights under this Section, We grant the person from whom We acquired those rights—You or the represented rights owner, as applicable—a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, transferable and sublicensable licence for their full duration and any extensions or renewals, irrevocable to the extent permitted by law, to do all acts listed in Section 2.1(b), for any purpose and under any terms that person chooses.

For rights licensed rather than assigned, that person retains equivalent freedom to use and license them notwithstanding the exclusivity in Section 2.1(b).

This licence back and retained freedom cover only the Contribution and rights received from that person. They do not grant rights in other parts of the Material, third-party rights or rights already owned by Us.

Individuals listed in Schedule A do not acquire the entity's licence back merely by being listed or by signing an Individual Contributor Assignment Agreement.

**(e) Independent undertaking.** The grants in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 are not subject to automatic termination or reversion for breach of Section 2.3. Section 2.3 is an independently enforceable contractual undertaking. Subject to mandatory law, its breach does not automatically divest title or terminate rights already granted. Remedies against Us, including damages and applications for injunctive or other equitable relief where available, remain available.

**(f) Confirmatory documents.** On Our reasonable request and at Our expense, You will execute documents reasonably necessary to confirm rights already granted under this Agreement. Where You made a grant for another rights owner, You will use reasonable efforts to procure any confirmation reasonably required from that owner. Such documents may not enlarge the obligations or rights granted. This is a confirmatory obligation, not a requirement for further permission before We upstream a Contribution.

### 2.3 Outbound Licensing Undertaking

**(a) Apache-2.0 undertaking.** In consideration of the grants and undertakings made under this Agreement, We undertake to You that We will license and sublicense Contributions covered by this undertaking only under the Apache License, Version 2.0, except for the licence back and retained freedom in Section 2.1(d), the ASF exception in Section 2.3(b), and transfers complying with Section 6.3.

No later version of the Apache License is automatically authorised.

Whenever We distribute a covered Contribution, We will make it available to the recipient under the Apache License, Version 2.0, and will not purport to withdraw or restrict those rights by separate terms.

We may charge for distributions and services. This undertaking does not require Us to publish a Contribution, provide source code where that licence does not require it, or accept any particular Contribution.

**(b) ASF exception.** We may grant to the ASF, and directly to recipients of software distributed by the ASF where its applicable instruments require, the copyright and patent licences and sublicences needed to contribute the Contribution for inclusion in an ASF project.

This includes grants under an ASF Software Grant Agreement or Corporate Contributor License Agreement and grants associated with submissions made by individuals under their own ASF Individual Contributor License Agreements. We may authorise the ASF's use, modification, distribution and sublicensing under those instruments, within the rights received under this Agreement, without obtaining further permission.

This exception authorises grants only for that ASF purpose. It does not permit Us to dual-license the Contribution generally on proprietary or other non-Apache terms, grant an independent non-Apache licence to an unrelated intermediary, or assign ownership outside Section 6.3.

The ASF's exercise of rights validly granted under its instruments is not controlled by Our undertaking in Section 2.3(a). This exception does not authorise Us to sign another person's contributor agreement in that person's name.

**(c) Scope.** This undertaking applies to Our exercise of rights received under this Agreement. It does not convert third-party material into Our property, alter its existing licence, or restrict an independent recipient's exercise of rights under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

We cannot avoid this undertaking merely by relying on an Apache licence to the same Contribution received from You.

**(d) Enforcement.** You may enforce this undertaking directly against Us. Section 5 does not restrict remedies for breach of this Section, Section 2.1(d) or Section 6.3. Protection on a transfer of rights is set out in Section 6.3. A change in Our shareholders does not change Our identity or release Us from this Agreement.

This Section does not purport to waive personal rights belonging to an author merely because You employ or engage that author. Author rights are addressed by the individuals' agreements and any other legally effective author consents or waivers You hold.

Nothing waives a right that applicable law does not permit its holder to waive or permits false attribution. This Section does not remove third-party attribution obligations.

Failure to obtain the written assumption is a breach of this Agreement, not an automatic reversal or invalidation of title. Nothing here represents that a contractual restriction binds every insolvency officeholder or purchaser by operation of property law.

Ordinary non-exclusive licences granted in compliance with Section 2.3, including ASF grants and a licence back, do not require a written assumption.

This Section does not restrict a beneficiary's use or transfer of its licence back. You may assign the benefit of this Agreement with Your relevant rights, subject to non-transferable personal rights.
